Judging criteria

What the judges score

Every pitch is evaluated live by our judge panel on three pillars.

📈

Traction

Proof it's real: customers, revenue, active users, or a working product already in the world. We reward progress, not promises.

🎯

Clarity of story

The problem, the insight, and why your team wins — told sharp enough to repeat. Judges score how clearly the business comes through.

🚀

Potential impact

Where it goes from here: the market, the growth path, and what a win unlocks for the venture and the community around it.

Final rubric and weights are announced at the event. Bring your cofounders — teams that tell the story together score together.

FAQ

Quick answers

Who can pitch?

Any currently enrolled Clemson University student founder and their cofounders. All majors welcome — builders, not just ideas.

What counts as a "real" business?

A venture with demonstrable traction — customers, revenue, a working product, or an active user base. Concept-only ideas and hypothetical ventures aren't eligible.

Can cofounders present together?

Yes — cofounder teams are strongly encouraged to pitch together.

Does Clemson Build take equity?

No. The competition is equity-free. You keep 100% of what you build.

Can I attend without pitching?

Absolutely. Spectators are welcome — RSVP to reserve a seat, watch the pitches, and meet the founders shaping Clemson's startup scene.

How do I sponsor or judge?

Email clemsonbuild@gmail.com — we'd love to talk.
